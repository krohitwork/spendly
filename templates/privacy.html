Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect your name, email address, and password. As you use the Service, we also store the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to operate the Service — authenticating your account, displaying your expenses back to you, and generating the category breakdowns and summaries you see in the app. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your account details and expense records are stored in Spendly's database and retained for as long as your account remains active. Passwords are stored in a hashed form and are never saved as plain text. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may rely on infrastructure providers, such as hosting services, solely to operate the Service, and these providers only process data on our behalf.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, you can reach out to us through your account settings or the support channels listed on our website.
